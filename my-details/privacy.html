Privacy for the My details page

Red Sun Studios, 94 Green St, Jamaica Plain, Boston MA. Run by Co-Green Street LLC with CoEverything. Last updated October 22, 2026.

What the page is for

My details lets a Red Sun Studios member see their own studio information: their studio, rent, balance, open invoices, recent payments, security deposit, keypad code, the doors it opens, and the building wifi. Each member sees only their own information.

What Google shares when you sign in

When you sign in with Google, Google tells us your name, your email address, and that the email is verified. We do not ask for access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts, calendar or anything else in your Google account.

We use your email only to find your row in our member records and show you that row. If your email isn't in our records, nothing is shown.

What we keep

  • We don't store your sign-in. Each time you sign in, the check happens and is forgotten.
  • The page doesn't use its own cookies, analytics or tracking, and doesn't save anything in your browser. Google's sign-in button and the fonts come from Google, which has its own privacy policy.
  • If you change your keypad code on the page, the new code goes straight to our door system (RemoteLock). We note that you changed your code and when, but not the code itself.

Who else is involved

Your member records live in a Google Sheet that only Co-Green Street staff can open, and door codes live in RemoteLock. We don't sell or share your information with anyone else.

Questions or changes

To ask what we have on file, correct it, or have your email removed, email ellie@coeverything.co.
